# The user guide of the device, for the model (tool device_guide): one topic per "[id] summary" line, its text
# below it. Written in English; the model says it in the user's language. Lines starting with # are comments.
# Never say the wake word aloud (it would wake the other BOX of the room up): call it "the wake word".

[overview] What the device is and everything it can do, in short
An ESP32-S3-BOX-3: a small screen with a speaker and microphones, a voice assistant answering through an AI model
of the cloud. It can: hold a spoken conversation (asked by the wake word, "Bonjour ESP" in French or "Hi ESP" in
English, or a touch on the screen); play internet radios; set timers, alarms and reminders; give the weather and
search the web; remember notes about the user; use the user's Google account (tasks and shopping list, mail,
agenda); drive a Homey home automation box and other MCP servers; play games out loud between several BOX; speak
six languages (French, English, Dutch, German, Spanish, Italian, others can be added) with many voices and models;
show the time, the costs, the Wi-Fi and the battery on its sleep screen; be set up from its Settings screen or from
files on a USB drive (configuration mode). Topics: conversation, gestures, radio, alarms, weather, memory,
google, homey, mcp, games, languages, models, voices, settings, screens, config, update, costs, troubleshooting.
For the exact phrase of a command, use the tool voice_commands.

[conversation] Talking to the assistant: waking it, follow-ups, ending, a new conversation, deep thinking
- Wake it with the wake word or a touch on the screen: a beep, "J'écoute" (listening) is shown; ask the question.
  After a short silence it is sent: "Entendu" (heard), then "Je réfléchis" (thinking), then the answer is shown
  and spoken sentence by sentence.
- After an answer it keeps listening 8 seconds without a beep: go on without the wake word.
- To interrupt an answer: say the wake word again, or double-tap the screen; it stops and listens.
- "Au revoir" / "Goodbye" said alone: back to sleep at once and the conversation is cleared. An answer ending
  with a goodbye also ends the conversation.
- The conversation is kept across sleep and restarts. After a pause, a new conversation starts unless the first
  phrase asks to go on with the previous one ("continue la conversation précédente").
- "Nouvelle discussion" / "New conversation": starts afresh without sleeping.
- "Abandon" / "Cancel": drops a question the device asked (which model, which town...) and listens again.
- Deep thinking (reasoning mode) can be switched on or off by voice for the models that reason: slower, better
  answers to hard questions.
- It knows the date and time with each question, and the user's country and town (for local answers).

[gestures] Touches on the screen
- Touch while asleep: wakes the assistant up, like the wake word.
- Double tap during an answer: stops it and listens.
- Double tap while listening or thinking (anywhere, the robot included): the question is dropped, back to sleep.
- Tap the Wi-Fi icon or the battery (bottom right of the sleep screen): the Wi-Fi and battery screen.
- Tap the volume (bottom left): the radio screen. Tap the time: the alarms and reminders screen.
- Tap the gear: the Settings screen; a long press on the gear: the System screen (firmware version, memory...).
- Tap the cost at the top right: the detailed costs.
- While a radio plays on the sleep screen, a double tap on the robot's head stops it.

[radio] Internet radio: stations, genres, favourites, the radio screen
- Played from the free Radio-Browser directory. Ask a station by its name ("joue la radio Nostalgie" / "play the
  radio Nostalgie"), a genre ("joue du jazz" / "play some jazz"), a country ("joue de la musique italienne"), or
  both. The model can also choose for free requests ("mets un truc calme pour dîner" / "something calm for
  dinner").
- While it plays: "suivante" / "next", "précédente" / "previous", "stop", "qu'est-ce qui passe" / "what's
  playing" (station and title); "remets la radio" / "resume the radio" plays it again. It pauses while the user
  talks to the assistant and comes back after.
- Favourites: "ajoute cette radio aux favoris" / "add this radio to my favourites", "retire cette radio des
  favoris", "joue mes favoris" / "play my favourites"; kept in RADIO.TXT on the configuration drive.
- The radio screen (tap the volume on the sleep screen): the station, the title, the buttons and the volume.
- The volume: "parle plus fort" / "louder", "baisse le son" / "quieter", "mets le volume à 50".

[alarms] Timers, alarms and reminders
- Asked in the user's own words: "minuteur de 10 minutes pour les pâtes" / "10-minute timer for the pasta",
  "réveille-moi à 7 h en semaine" / "wake me at 7 on weekdays", "réveille-moi demain à 7 h avec Classic 21" (an
  alarm with a radio), "rappelle-moi jeudi à 15 h d'appeler Paul" / "remind me Thursday at 3 pm to call Paul".
- Repetitions: every day, weekdays, the weekend, chosen days, every month (same day, "the second Tuesday", "the
  last Friday", the last day), every year (birthdays).
- When due: a chime (or the radio) rising from silence, then its label said. A reminder: a short chime and its
  text. Stop it with a touch or the wake word; then "encore 5 minutes" / "5 more minutes" rings it again later.
  Not stopped, it stops by itself after 3 minutes.
- Ask what is set ("quelles alarmes ai-je ?", "combien de temps reste-t-il ?"), delete one ("supprime l'alarme de
  7 h"). On the sleep screen, letters near the time show what is set, a timer's countdown is shown at the top;
  tap the time for the list.
- Kept across restarts; a reminder missed during a power cut is said once the clock is back.

[weather] Weather, news and web search
- The weather now and for up to 7 days, anywhere (Open-Meteo): "quel temps fait-il à Namur ?", "va-t-il pleuvoir
  demain ?". Without a place, the user's town (set by "change ma ville" / "change my town", or City in CONFIG.INI).
- Current information (news, results, hours, prices) by a web search: the models of providers with their own
  search, or Perplexity's search for the others when a Perplexity key is set.

[memory] Long-term memory notes
- "Souviens-toi que ..." / "Remember that ...": a note used in every answer from now on.
- "Oublie que ..." / "Forget that ...", "oublie tout" / "forget everything", "dis-moi ce que tu as mémorisé" /
  "what do you remember?".
- The notes are in MEMORY.TXT on the configuration drive, editable there.
- Who the assistant is (its name, personality, how it speaks) is in PERSO_<XX>.TXT, one per language.

[google] Google account: Tasks and shopping list, Gmail, Calendar
- Lists and tasks ("ajoute du lait à la liste de courses" / "add milk to the shopping list", "qu'est-ce qu'il
  reste sur ma liste ?"), mails ("ai-je des nouveaux mails ?", read, summarize), agenda ("qu'est-ce que j'ai
  demain ?", add an event).
- Setting it up (once): a Google Cloud project of the user with the Tasks, Gmail and Calendar APIs and an OAuth
  client of type "Desktop app"; its client id and secret go into CONFIG.INI (Google_client, Google_secret); then
  the account is linked from the configuration mode (a link to open on the PC, the code given back). The README of
  the project explains each step.
- The current state is in the "status" of this tool's result.

[homey] Home automation with a Homey Pro
- Switch devices on and off, set them, activate moods, start flows, tell who is home and the energy used: "allume
  la lumière du salon", "active l'ambiance cinéma", "lance le flow Bonne nuit", "qui est à la maison ?".
- Setting it up: install the MCP AI Bridge app on the Homey, then add it in CONFIG.INI as an MCP server named Homey
  (Mcp1_name = Homey, Mcp1_url = its address, Mcp1_key = its key), and restart.
- Only with the models that take tools.

[mcp] Other MCP servers (tools for the model)
- Any MCP server reachable over HTTP (a home server, a local service, an online one with a key): its tools are
  offered to the model, which uses them when a question needs them.
- Added in CONFIG.INI, section [MCP]: McpN_name, McpN_url, McpN_key (optional), McpN_tools (to choose its tools),
  McpN_on. The MCP servers page of the Settings screen shows each one (tools, connecting, unreachable, off) with a
  box to switch it on or off.

[games] Games played out loud between several BOX
- Two BOX or more on the same network take turns out loud, each with its own voice, hearing each other with their
  microphones. Say the phrase of a game to one BOX (after the wake word): the others join by themselves.
- The games: a debate (a fight of words on a topic), a riddle contest, a collaborative creation (an idea invented
  together, its requirements written to PROJECT.TXT on the configuration drive), "who am I?" (a famous person to
  find by yes/no questions), Akinator (a person, an object, an animal or a film to find), "higher or lower" (a
  number or a year to find), the trial (a prosecutor and a lawyer plead an absurd case, the judge's verdict at
  the end).
- A touch on any BOX stops the game on all. A guessing game ends with the answer revealed.
- Their phrases: the tool voice_commands. The number of lines: Game_turns_max in CONFIG.INI; the games can be
  changed or new ones added in GAMES.INI on the configuration drive.

[languages] Languages
- Six languages given: French, English, Dutch, German, Spanish, Italian: the screen, the transcription, the
  answers and the voice. Switched by voice ("parle anglais" / "speak French", "change la langue") or from the
  Settings screen.
- Another language can be added in CONFIG.INI (Languages); the device then makes its files (LANG_, VOICE_,
  PERSO_<XX>.TXT) from the English ones, to be translated on the configuration drive.

[models] The AI models answering
- Claude (Anthropic, the default), GPT (OpenAI), Gemini (Google), Grok (xAI), Mistral, Perplexity (with web
  search), and many models through OpenRouter, free ones included. Each provider needs its API key in CONFIG.INI.
- Change by voice ("change de modèle" / "change the model", "utilise le modèle 3" / "use model 3") or from the
  Settings screen; the models offered are listed in AGENTS.INI, and can be ticked or unticked in the Settings.
- "Teste les modèles" / "test the models" measures how fast each one answers; "quels sont les modèles les plus
  rapides ?" gives the best three.
- The transcription (STT) and the speech (TTS) models are chosen the same way ("change de transcription", "change
  de synthèse vocale").

[voices] Voices
- Voices of OpenAI, Mistral (natural voices per language, with an emotion or a pace), Deepgram Aura-2 (also through
  OpenRouter), ElevenLabs, depending on the keys set. "Change de voix" / "change the voice", "prends la voix
  Cedar"; or the Settings screen. The new voice introduces itself.
- A voice may refuse to say some words (Mistral blocks insults): another voice then.

[settings] The Settings screen on the device
- Opened by the gear of the sleep screen; eight pages: language and model; transcription, speech and voice; the
  LLM, STT and TTS models (ticked or not, a long press tests one); the MCP servers; the beep after a reply; the
  debug traces.
- The folder-and-gear icon opens the configuration mode. Two icons there reset the device: the soft reset (the
  settings and the texts back to the firmware's, Wi-Fi, keys and MCP servers kept) and the factory reset
  (everything erased).
- The clock on the sleep screen: "affiche l'heure" / "cache l'heure". The country and town: "change mon pays",
  "change ma ville". The firmware version: "quelle est ta version ?". A restart by voice too.

[screens] What the screens show
- The sleep screen: the robot asleep, the time and date (if shown), the volume, the Wi-Fi signal, the battery with
  the BOX-3-SENSOR dock (whose presence sensor also switches the screen off when nobody is there), the costs.
- Listening, thinking and answering: the robot, the words heard, the answer scrolling.
- The radio screen, the alarms screen, the Wi-Fi and battery screen, the System screen, the Settings screen.

[config] The configuration mode and the files of the USB drive
- Opened by "édite la config" / "edit the config", or Settings, folder-and-gear icon. The BOX restarts and shows up
  on the PC as a USB drive named ESP-BOX.
- Its files: CONFIG.INI (Wi-Fi, API keys, settings, MCP servers, Google), MEMORY.TXT (memory notes), AGENTS.INI
  (models and voices offered), LANG_<XX>.TXT (texts of the device), VOICE_<XX>.TXT (phrases of the voice
  commands), PERSO_<XX>.TXT (who the assistant is), COUNTRY.TXT, RADIO.TXT (favourite radios), GAMES.INI (games),
  PROJECT.TXT (the last collaborative creation), COSTS.TXT and SPEED.TXT (written by the device), DEBUG.TXT,
  BOOTS.TXT (the last starts).
- Edit, save, then wait about 10 seconds (longer for a big file) before pressing Restart on the screen.
- An emptied file comes back as the firmware's version at the next start.

[update] Updating the firmware
- From the releases of the project on GitHub: three small files (partitions, app, config) flashed with
  esptool-js in a browser, without erasing anything; or the full image (erases the settings).
- After an update, the new texts and phrases are added to the existing files; the soft reset gives the new
  versions of all of them.

[costs] Costs
- Each provider is paid by use (except the free models). The cost of the conversation and of the day are shown at
  the top right of the screen; a tap opens the details per kind (transcription, model, speech) and per month.
  Everything is counted in COSTS.TXT.

[troubleshooting] When something goes wrong
- A missing credit, a refused key or a network problem is said and shown, with the provider.
- No Wi-Fi or no key: the device starts in configuration mode; fill CONFIG.INI.
- Not understood: speak closer; a phrase of a command not recognised can be added to VOICE_<XX>.TXT.
- The debug traces (Settings, last page) can be dumped to DEBUG.TXT for the developer.
